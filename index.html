Our Craft

Woven with patience,
finished by hand

"A shirt cut in 100-thread linen should feel better at five years than at five minutes."

Every Meridian garment begins at the loom. We work directly with seven family-run mills across Portugal, Japan, and Ireland — places where the vocabulary of fabric is still passed person to person, season to season.

We commission yarn weights and weave structures the market doesn't offer off the shelf, then cut and finish in our Lisbon atelier. The result is cloth that drapes, breathes, and wears in — rather than wearing out.

How We Work

From field to finished cloth

01

Source

We identify farms and mills that share our standards — traceability from raw fibre, fair pay throughout, and no synthetic shortcuts.

02

Commission

Custom yarn weights and weave structures — nothing off the shelf. Each season we develop constructions specific to the garments we have in mind.

03

Construct

Cut and assembled in our Lisbon atelier by 24 artisan tailors, each trained for a minimum of three years before touching a finished garment.

04

Finish

Every seam pressed. Every button hand-sewn. Every piece inspected before dispatch. No exceptions, regardless of volume or deadline.
